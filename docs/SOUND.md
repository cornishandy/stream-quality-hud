# Best streaming sound per service and Mac browser

Researched 18 Oct 2026 (America/Indiana/Indianapolis) by GPT-6.1-Sol. Service-owned help is used throughout; official product FAQs and press are explicitly labelled. Numbered source extracts were checked today. Some help sites expose only a JavaScript shell to direct retrieval: sources marked **indexed text** were checked in the search index of that exact official page, not independently rendered live. No browser window, account, playback, forum evidence, or codec-based inference was used.

Caps describe what the **service documents**, conditional on the title, plan, audio track and compatible equipment specified below. **unknown** / `""` means no Mac-browser audio format was verified in the cited official material; it does not mean stereo or no support. Safari figures are **Safari-only**. Orion is unknown throughout. Device examples are not exhaustive. “Best anywhere” lists the richest documented format, not a guarantee for every title or country; a regional source is explicitly identified.
